Case Study 01

Pink Chicken NY

Buying, Merchandising & E-Commerce Intern — Summer 2026
The Role

Supported the Buying, Merchandising, and E-Commerce teams at Pink Chicken, a contemporary children's and women's fashion brand — managing seasonal product assortments and optimizing the online customer experience.

The Process

Work moved across three connected functions on two different timelines: on e-commerce, keeping pace with current launches — maintaining and merchandising product live on the site in real time. On buying, working ahead — supporting assortment planning for Fall 2026, Holiday 2026, Spring 2027, and Fall 2027 as a forward-looking intern project, paired with competitive market research into trends, pricing, and assortment gaps. On merchandising, analyzing sales, inventory, and sell-through reports to guide replenishment decisions across both timelines.

The Work
  • Assisted buyers with seasonal assortment planning across four future seasons — Fall 2026, Holiday 2026, Spring 2027, and Fall 2027 — as a forward-looking intern buying project
  • Conducted competitive market research on trends, pricing, and assortment opportunities
  • Analyzed sales, inventory, and sell-through reports to support replenishment and merchandising decisions
  • Maintained product information, imagery, and merchandising content across the e-commerce platform
  • Created and updated product collections, category pages, and merchandising features
  • Supported promotional campaigns, launches, and site merchandising during key retail events
  • Collaborated cross-functionally with design, marketing, operations, and production
  • Prepared reports and presentations in Excel to support business planning
Results: Buying direction contributed to assortment planning used across multiple future seasons, and the merchandising work completed on the online store is seen by thousands of shoppers daily.

Case Study 02

Freddy B Boutique

E-Commerce & Social Content Styling
The Role

Styling and content creation for a boutique retailer's e-commerce and social channels, including a buying trip to the Free People buying office in NYC to curate Freddy B's Spring 2026 collection.

The Process

Every new shipment started with a plan, not a shoot. I built style mood boards using flat lays of incoming inventory, mapping outfit combinations and shot lists before styling and shooting each look — keeping weekly content consistent and on-brand under tight turnaround.

The Work
  • Curated roughly 20 styles for Freddy B's Spring 2026 collection at the Free People buying office, NYC
  • Styled and photographed looks for weekly e-commerce and social shoots
  • Built pre-shoot mood boards to plan cohesive collections before styling
Results: The pre-shoot mood board process sped up photoshoot days and made the shoot easier for the photographer to execute, by mapping every look and shot in advance rather than deciding on set.
